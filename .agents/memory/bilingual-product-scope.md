---
name: Bilingual product scope
description: Whole-site Arabic/English continuity and direction requirements.
---

The language choice belongs to the whole TEAK experience, including the maintenance entry page, employee services, and administrative interfaces. Arabic must use RTL and English LTR; do not treat translation as a maintenance-page-only feature.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested language switching from the beginning of the site and on every page, including correct layout direction.

**How to apply:** New standalone pages and new application screens must participate in the same saved choice. Translate interface copy, not official employee names, entered notes, credentials, or document contents. Preserve maintenance access boundaries when exposing language assets.
