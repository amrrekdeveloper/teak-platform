BUILD (React Three Fiber, reuse the project's existing three/@react-three/fiber/drei setup):

1. ROOM FROM CUSTOMER DATA
- Build the room from the store's wall dimensions (cm → meters, 1 unit = 1m) and ceiling height.
- Walls with real thickness (~12cm) and REAL cut openings: each door/window is an actual hole (build each wall from box segments around the openings — left/right segments, segment above door, segments above/below window). No fake decals.
- Floor sized to room footprint. Ceiling: omit or single-sided facing down so it never blocks the camera/light.

2. PBR MATERIALS
- Floor: laminate textures (diff as map with sRGB colorSpace, nor_gl as normalMap, rough as roughnessMap). RepeatWrapping, tile roughly every 1m for real-scale planks.
- Walls: painted plaster (diff sRGB, nor_gl normalMap, arm as packed AO/Roughness/Metalness map on the respective channels).
- Countertop material (oak veneer) prepared the same way for later use.

3. LIGHTING & REALISM (the critical quality bar — IKEA planner level)
- HDR via RGBELoader / drei Environment (files prop) as environment lighting.
- Renderer: ACESFilmicToneMapping, outputColorSpace SRGB, soft shadows (PCFSoft or drei SoftShadows).
- One directional light (sun-like through windows) casting soft shadows, tuned to complement the HDRI without blowing out.
- All meshes castShadow/receiveShadow appropriately.

4. CABINET GLB
- Load /kitchen-tool-assets/cabinet-lower-standard.glb with useGLTF.
- SCALE FIX: measure its bounding box after loading and uniformly rescale so height is exactly 0.9m; place it standing on the floor against a sensible default wall (based on kitchen shape). castShadow/receiveShadow.

5. UTILITIES DISPLAY (from the store — show each placed utility at its true position)
- Water supply: small 3D marker on the wall at its stored position and height (e.g. blue disc/badge with faucet feel; a simple extruded cylinder + icon sprite is fine).
- Outlets and switches: small white rounded plates on the walls at stored positions/heights (outlet at 30cm default, switch at 110cm) — simple box geometry with subtle material, readable but discreet.
- Floor drains: dark circular disc slightly above the floor surface (avoid z-fighting) at stored x/y. Wall drains: small circle on the wall at stored height.
- These markers are visual only in this phase (no interaction), must not cast harsh shadows (castShadow false is fine), and must never block the camera.

6. CAMERA & CONTROLS
- PerspectiveCamera at human-ish eye level, OrbitControls (drei) with limits: maxPolarAngle slightly under 90° (never below floor), sensible min/max distance, target near room center at ~1m height.

7. INTEGRATION
